We recommend the TFIDF Cosine method because this provides the most recommendations of songs with a matching
genere to the reference song. Compared to the raw dot and raw cosine methods, this method has 117 hits as
opposed to 2 and 65, respectively.

One limitation of lyric word counts alone is that the content of the song is not what is being valued as 
much as the length of the song. This is because the raw dot and raw cosine methods do not ignore filler-words.
Instead, those two methods reward them, rather than just focusing on words with substance or words that are less common.

To make the system better, we could compare the names of the songs in addition to the TFIDF Cosine method.
